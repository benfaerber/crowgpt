# What happens to your recordings

## What you send

Nothing leaves your phone until you sync. When you do, the app uploads:

- the **audio** (and video, if you recorded video) of each capture;
- **where it was**, only as precisely as you choose: exact GPS, within a few
  miles, or just a region you choose. At region precision no coordinates are
  sent at all;
- **what you noted**: call labels, context tags (a dog nearby, food put down,
  a new crow arriving), notes, and the crows and families you name;
- **spoken notes** you dictate, and their transcripts;
- **app details** (app version, phone model, error logs) so broken syncs can
  be fixed.

## What it is used for

Uploading means your recordings help build:

1. **CawDex**, a catalogue and classifier of crow call types.
2. **Crow Translate**, which pairs calls with what was happening when they
   were made. Your context tags are the most valuable thing you send.

And, only if you allow it:

3. **The generator**, a model that produces new crow calls.

## Who sees it

- Your recordings are stored on the CrowGPT server and seen by the people who
  run the project. Other app users cannot see them.
- Your exact location is never shown publicly, whatever precision you chose.
- Things built *from* recordings, like trained models, statistics and generated
  calls, may be published. They don't contain your audio or your location.

## Publishing

Recordings are **not published today**. CrowGPT is open source, and one day
the recordings may be released as a public dataset for research. That happens
**only if you opt in**, and only to recordings made in this app:

- Location goes out at your chosen precision or coarser, never finer.
- Spoken notes, their transcripts, and any recording where people can be heard
  talking are never published.
- You are credited by the name you choose, or anonymously if you prefer.
- You will be told the licence before anything of yours is released.

## Changing your mind

- You can change your answers at any time in Settings. A change applies to
  recordings you upload from then on.
- To delete recordings from the server, or your whole account, email
  hello@crowgpt.org.
- A model already trained on a recording can't un-learn it, but deleted
  recordings are left out of every model trained afterwards, and out of the
  next dataset release.

## If this policy changes

Recordings are used under the policy you accepted when you uploaded them. A
new version applies only to what you upload after accepting it, and the app
will ask you first.
