Talk to CrowGPT

A small transformer, 18.7M parameters, that learned to sound like a crow from call bouts cut out of archive recordings, with no human labeling anywhere in the loop. Ask it for a call and it makes a brand-new one. Nothing it says is a recording of a bird.

  1. Caw. I'm CrowGPT. I learned to talk by listening to thousands of crow recordings, and nobody labelled a single one for me.
  2. Ask me for a call below and I'll make one for you on the spot, with its own seed, so nobody else hears the same crow.

Waking the crow…

Please don't play these outdoors. A fake alarm call could stress real crows, so I don't play them to birds either, and the model's access terms carry the same rule.

How it compares

On a crow detector, an earlier 18M version of CrowGPT scored well ahead of a 433M pretrained audio model fine-tuned on the same corpus. That detector also chose the training data, which flatters the lead by an unknown amount, so a listening test gets the final word.
